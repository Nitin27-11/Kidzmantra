1 | 🍎 🦉 🏫 | Nouns, and the little words a, an and the!
1.2 aarav-think | Why do we say "an apple" but "a ball"?
1.2 buddy-happy | We use "an" before a vowel sound – a, e, i, o, u. Otherwise "a".
1.3 riya-wow | So it's "an owl" and "an umbrella"!
1.4 buddy-happy | And "the" points to one particular thing, like "the sun".
2 | 👦 👧 🎒 | Pronouns help us!
2.2 riya-happy | I, you, he, she, it, we, they – personal pronouns!
2.2 buddy-happy | Yes! And mine, yours, his, hers show who owns something.
2.4 aarav-think | "This bag is mine" – not "This bag is me"!
3 | ⏪ ⏺️ ⏩ | Verbs tell time too!
3.2 aarav-think | How does a verb show time?
3.2 buddy-happy | It changes! I play (present), I played (past), I will play (future).
3.4 riya-happy | He plays, she sings, it runs – add -s for he, she and it!
4 | 🐘 🐁 🏆 | Adjectives describe!
4.2 riya-happy | Five red roses! "Five" and "red" are adjectives!
4.2 buddy-happy | Right! Adjectives tell what kind, how many and which one.
4.3 aarav-wow | Tall, taller, tallest! Adjectives can compare too!
5 | ❓ ❗ ✏️ | Punctuation marks!
5.2 aarav-think | What's the use of a full stop?
5.2 buddy-happy | It shows where a sentence ends. Commas, question marks and more help us read clearly.
5.3 riya-wow | Without punctuation, sentences get so confusing!
6 | ❓ 💬 🗣️ | Sentences and questions!
6.2 riya-happy | "Close the door" is an order – an imperative sentence!
6.2 buddy-happy | Yes! There are telling, asking, ordering and exclaiming sentences.
6.4 aarav-think | "You are happy" becomes "Are you happy?" – just swap the words!
7 | 🐜 🦗 ❄️ | The Ant and the Grasshopper!
7.2 aarav-think | Why does the ant work hard in summer?
7.2 buddy-happy | Read the passage to find out! The answer is in the story.
7.3 riya-happy | We should work today to be ready for tomorrow!
8 | ✉️ 📮 👵 | Let's write a letter!
8.2 riya-happy | I want to write to my Dadi in Jaipur!
8.2 buddy-happy | Lovely! An informal letter has an address, date, greeting, body and ending.
8.3 aarav-wow | I'll end mine with "Your loving grandson"!
9 | 🌧️ 🐸 ☔ | Poems about rain!
9.2 aarav-wow | Pitter-patter! I can hear the rain in the poem!
9.2 buddy-happy | Pitter-patter is a sound word. It copies the sound of rain.
9.4 riya-happy | I read it aloud with expression, slowly and clearly!
