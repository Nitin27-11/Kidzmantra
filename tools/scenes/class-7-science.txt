1 | 🌿 ☀️ 🍃 | How do plants get food?
1.1 riya-think | Plants don't eat like us. How do they get food?
1.1 buddy-happy | They make it themselves! Plants are autotrophs.
1.2 aarav-wow | Leaves use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food – photosynthesis!
1.3 riya-think | The insect-eating pitcher plant – is it a plant or an animal?
2 | 🍎 🫃 🐄 | Nutrition in animals!
2.2 aarav-think | How long is our small intestine?
2.2 buddy-happy | About 7.5 metres – coiled up inside us!
2.4 riya-wow | Cows chew the cud – they bring food back up to chew again!
3 | 🔥 🌡️ 🧥 | Heat!
3.2 riya-think | Why does a steel spoon in hot tea get hot?
3.2 buddy-happy | Heat travels through it by conduction. Metals are good conductors.
3.4 aarav-happy | We wear dark clothes in winter because they absorb more heat!
4 | 🍋 🧼 🧪 | Acids, bases and salts!
4.1 aarav-wow | Lemons taste sour because they're acidic!
4.2 buddy-happy | Indicators like litmus show us: acids turn blue litmus red, bases turn red litmus blue.
4.3 riya-happy | An antacid neutralises extra acid in the stomach!
5 | 🧊 🔩 🍳 | Physical and chemical changes!
5.1 riya-think | Is melting ice a chemical change?
5.1 buddy-happy | No – it's physical. It can be reversed, and no new substance forms.
5.3 aarav-happy | Painting iron stops rust from forming!
6 | ❄️ 🌴 🐧 | Weather, climate and adaptation!
6.1 aarav-think | Weather and climate – aren't they the same?
6.1 buddy-happy | Weather is day-to-day. Climate is the pattern over about 25 years.
6.2 riya-wow | Polar bears have white fur and a thick layer of fat to stay warm!
7 | 🌬️ 🌪️ ⛈️ | Winds, storms and cyclones!
7.1 riya-think | Can air really push things?
7.1 buddy-happy | Yes! Air exerts pressure. Wind is air moving from high to low pressure.
7.5 aarav-happy | During a storm, stay indoors and away from trees and poles!
8 | 🪱 🌾 🏞️ | Soil!
8.1 aarav-wow | Soil has layers – like a cake!
8.2 buddy-happy | And types: sandy, clayey and loamy. Loamy soil is best for most crops.
8.4 riya-think | Tree roots hold the soil and stop erosion!
9 | 🫁 🏃 🐟 | Respiration!
9.2 riya-think | Why do my legs hurt after running fast?
9.2 buddy-happy | Without enough oxygen, muscles respire anaerobically and make lactic acid.
9.4 aarav-wow | Fish breathe through gills!
10 | 🫀 🩸 🌳 | Transport in animals and plants!
10.1 aarav-happy | My heart beats about 72 times a minute!
10.2 buddy-happy | Blood carries oxygen and food. Kidneys clean waste from the blood.
10.3 riya-wow | Xylem carries water up a tree, even 100 metres high!
11 | 🌸 🐝 🌰 | Reproduction in plants!
11.1 riya-think | Can a plant grow without a seed?
11.1 buddy-happy | Yes! A potato grows from its eyes, a rose from a cutting – asexual reproduction.
11.3 aarav-happy | Bees carry pollen from flower to flower – pollination!
12 | 🚗 ⏱️ 📈 | Motion and time!
12.1 aarav-think | Who's faster – the one who covers more distance or takes less time?
12.1 buddy-happy | Speed tells us! Speed = distance ÷ time.
12.4 riya-happy | A speedometer shows speed, an odometer shows distance!
13 | 💡 🔋 🧲 | Electric current and its effects!
13.2 riya-wow | A heater gets hot because of electric current!
13.3 buddy-happy | That's the heating effect. A fuse melts and breaks the circuit if too much current flows.
13.4 aarav-happy | A current-carrying wire acts like a magnet – electromagnet!
14 | 🔦 🪞 🌈 | Light!
14.1 aarav-think | Why can't I see around a corner?
14.1 buddy-happy | Because light travels in straight lines!
14.6 riya-wow | White sunlight splits into seven colours – that's a rainbow!
15 | 💧 🌍 🚰 | Water – a precious resource!
15.1 riya-think | If Earth is mostly water, why is there a shortage?
15.1 buddy-happy | Most of it is salty seawater. Very little is fresh water we can use.
15.5 aarav-happy | Drip irrigation saves water on farms!
16 | 🌳 🐅 🍄 | Forests – our lifeline!
16.1 aarav-wow | Forests are home to so many plants and animals!
16.2 buddy-happy | Grass → deer → tiger. That's a food chain!
16.4 riya-think | Cutting forests causes floods and less rain!
17 | 🚿 🏭 ♻️ | Wastewater!
17.1 riya-think | Where does water go after we use it?
17.2 buddy-happy | It's cleaned at a wastewater treatment plant before going back to rivers.
17.3 aarav-happy | I won't pour oil down the sink – it blocks pipes!
