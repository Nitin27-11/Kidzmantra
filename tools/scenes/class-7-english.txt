1 | ⏪ ⏺️ ⏩ | A quick tour of tenses!
1.1 aarav-think | There are so many tenses. How do I choose the right one?
1.1 buddy-happy | Ask two questions: when did it happen, and is it complete, ongoing or both?
1.2 riya-happy | He said that he was tired – the reported verb moves back in time!
1.3 aarav-wow | "I have seen him yesterday" is wrong? It's "I saw him yesterday"!
2 | 🧩 🔗 📝 | Clauses build sentences!
2.1 riya-think | What's the difference between a clause and a phrase?
2.1 buddy-happy | A clause has a subject and a verb. A phrase doesn't.
2.4 aarav-happy | "I stayed home because it was raining." Two clauses joined!
3 | 🔄 🏏 📦 | Active and passive voice!
3.1 aarav-happy | "Kohli hit the ball" becomes "The ball was hit by Kohli"!
3.2 buddy-happy | Even questions and commands can be passive: "Let the door be closed."
3.3 riya-think | "He gave me a pen" has two objects – so two passives are possible!
4 | 💬 🗣️ 📰 | Reported speech!
4.1 riya-happy | Aarav said, "I am hungry." → Aarav said that he was hungry.
4.3 buddy-happy | When the reporting verb is in the past, tenses move one step back.
4.5 aarav-think | Commands become "told me to…" – "Sit down" → She told me to sit down!
5 | 🌧️ ☁️ 🌾 | Read like a detective!
5.1 aarav-think | How can I read faster and still understand?
5.1 buddy-happy | Skim for the main idea first, then scan for details when you answer.
5.3 riya-wow | Nearly half of India's farmland still depends on monsoon rain!
6 | 📔 ✉️ 🖊️ | Diaries and letters!
6.1 riya-happy | I write my diary every night before bed!
6.1 buddy-happy | A diary entry has a date, a personal tone and your feelings.
6.3 aarav-happy | An informal letter to a friend can be chatty and warm!
7 | 📖 🐉 ✍️ | Let's write stories!
7.1 aarav-think | What makes a story interesting?
7.1 buddy-happy | Strong characters, a problem to solve, rising tension and a satisfying ending.
7.3 riya-happy | Show, don't tell – "her hands shook" instead of "she was scared"!
8 | 🛤️ 🍂 🌲 | The Road Not Taken!
8.2 riya-think | Is the poem really about two roads in a wood?
8.3 buddy-happy | It's about the choices we make in life, and how they shape who we become.
8.4 aarav-wow | The two roads are a metaphor!
