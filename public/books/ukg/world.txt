## My body
Our body has many parts. Each part has a special job.

SAY: riya-think | What is inside my body?
SAY: buddy-happy | Your heart, lungs, bones and brain work inside you all day!

## Outer parts
Head, hair, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, neck, shoulders, arms, hands, fingers, stomach, legs, knees, feet and toes.

## Inside parts
- The **heart** pumps blood. Put your hand on your chest and feel it beat! ❤️
- The **lungs** help us breathe.
- The **brain** helps us think and remember. 🧠
- **Bones** hold our body up.

SAY: aarav-happy | Exercise and good food keep my body healthy!

## Keep your body healthy
- Bathe every day and brush your teeth twice.
- Eat fruits and vegetables.
- Play outside and sleep early.
> 💡 We have 10 fingers and 10 toes.

## Let's check
Q: Which part helps us think?
A: The brain.
Q: Which part pumps blood?
A: The heart.
Q: How many times should we brush our teeth each day?
A: Twice.

## My family
A family is a group of people who live together and care for each other.

SAY: aarav-happy | My family is big. Dada, Dadi, Chacha and cousins live with us!
SAY: buddy-happy | Some families are big, some are small. Both are full of love!

## Small and big families
- A **small family** has parents and their children.
- A **big family** (joint family) also has grandparents, uncles, aunts and cousins.

## Family members
- Father's father is **Dadaji**; father's mother is **Dadiji**.
- Mother's father is **Nanaji**; mother's mother is **Naniji**.
- Father's brother is **Chachaji**; mother's brother is **Mamaji**.

## We help each other
We share work at home: laying the table, watering plants, folding clothes.
> 💡 Spend time with grandparents. They know wonderful stories!

SAY: riya-happy | I help Dadi water the plants.

## Let's check
Q: What is a joint family?
A: A big family with grandparents, uncles, aunts and cousins.
Q: What do we call our mother's brother?
A: Mamaji.

Just like us, animals need homes. Different animals live in different places.

Frogs live both on land and in water.
> 💡 Never disturb a bird's nest or a beehive.

## Fruits and vegetables
Fruits and vegetables give us **vitamins**. Vitamins help us grow and keep us from falling sick.

SAY: aarav-happy | A banana is yellow and a grape is green!
SAY: buddy-happy | Fruits come in many colours. Eat a rainbow of fruits!

## Fruits by colour
- Red: apple, strawberry, pomegranate
- Yellow: banana, mango, pineapple
- Green: guava, grapes, pear
- Orange: orange, papaya

## Vegetables by part
- **Roots** we eat: carrot, radish, beetroot.
- **Stems** we eat: potato (it grows under the ground).
- **Leaves** we eat: spinach, cabbage, coriander.
- **Fruits** we cook as vegetables: tomato, brinjal, lady's finger.
> 💡 Eat a "rainbow plate" – many colours of fruits and vegetables every day.

SAY: riya-wow | A carrot is a root, and spinach is a leaf!

## Let's check
Q: Name two leafy vegetables.
A: Spinach and cabbage (or coriander).
Q: Name a red fruit.
A: Apple, strawberry or pomegranate.
Q: Which part of the carrot do we eat?
A: The root.

## Colours
Colours make our world bright and beautiful.

SAY: riya-think | What happens if I mix red and yellow?
SAY: buddy-happy | You get orange! Red, yellow and blue are primary colours.

## Primary colours
**Red, yellow and blue** are primary colours. We can mix them to make new colours.

## Mixing colours
- Red + Yellow = **Orange** 🟠
- Yellow + Blue = **Green** 🟢
- Red + Blue = **Purple** 🟣
- Red + White = **Pink**
- Black + White = **Grey**

SAY: aarav-happy | Our flag has saffron, white and green!

## Colours of our flag
India's flag has **saffron**, **white** and **green**, with a **navy blue** wheel in the middle. 🇮🇳
> 💡 A rainbow has seven colours: violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange, red.

## Let's check
Q: What do red and yellow make?
A: Orange.
Q: Name the three primary colours.
A: Red, yellow and blue.
Q: What colour is the wheel on India's flag?
A: Navy blue.

## Weather and seasons
**Weather** is how the air outside is today: sunny, rainy, windy or cold.

SAY: aarav-happy | I love the rainy season! I jump in puddles!
SAY: buddy-happy | India has summer, rainy season and winter.

## Seasons in India
- **Summer** – hot days. We wear cotton clothes and drink lots of water. ☀️
- **Rainy season (monsoon)** – rain falls. We use umbrellas and raincoats. 🌧️
- **Winter** – cold days. We wear sweaters and caps. ❄️
- **Spring** – flowers bloom and the weather is pleasant. 🌸

## Weather words
Sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, foggy, stormy.
> 💡 Never stand under a tree during a thunderstorm.

SAY: riya-think | Today is sunny and hot. I will wear a cap!

## Let's check
Q: In which season do we wear sweaters?
A: Winter.
Q: In which season do we use umbrellas most?
A: The rainy season.
Q: What clothes are best in summer?
A: Cotton clothes.

## Good habits
Good habits keep us healthy, happy and safe.

SAY: riya-happy | I brush my teeth every morning and night!
SAY: buddy-happy | Good habits keep us healthy, kind and safe.

## Healthy habits
- Wake up early and brush your teeth.
- Wash your hands with soap before eating and after using the toilet. 🧼
- Eat healthy food; say no to too much junk food.
- Drink clean water.
- Sleep on time.

## Kind habits
- Say please, thank you and sorry.
- Respect elders.
- Share and help others.

## Safe habits
- Do not talk to strangers.
- Do not play with fire or electric plugs.
> 💡 Wash hands for 20 seconds – as long as singing "Happy Birthday" twice!

SAY: aarav-happy | I share my toys with my friends!

## Let's check
Q: When should we wash our hands?
A: Before eating and after using the toilet.
Q: Should we talk to strangers?
A: No.
Q: Name one kind habit.
A: Sharing, helping, or saying please and thank you.
