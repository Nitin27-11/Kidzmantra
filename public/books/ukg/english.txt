## The alphabet A to Z
The **alphabet** is all 26 letters in order. Every letter has a capital and a small form.

## Capital and small together
Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh Ii Jj Kk Ll Mm
Nn Oo Pp Qq Rr Ss Tt Uu Vv Ww Xx Yy Zz

## Vowels and consonants
- **Vowels:** a, e, i, o, u (5 letters).
- **Consonants:** all the other 21 letters.

## Letter order
- The letter **after** d is **e**.
- The letter **before** k is **j**.
- The letter **between** p and r is **q**.
> 💡 Sing the alphabet song to remember the order.

## Let's check
Q: How many vowels are there?
A: Five: a, e, i, o, u.
Q: Which letter comes between s and u?
A: t.
Q: Is "b" a vowel or a consonant?
A: A consonant.

@@ 2
## Letter sounds (phonics)
Each letter makes a sound. When we blend sounds, we read words!

## Short vowel sounds
- **a** as in c**a**t, m**a**t, b**a**g
- **e** as in h**e**n, p**e**n, b**e**d
- **i** as in p**i**g, s**i**t, l**i**d
- **o** as in d**o**g, p**o**t, b**o**x
- **u** as in s**u**n, c**u**p, b**u**s

## Blending
Say the sounds and slide them together:
- b – a – t → **bat**
- h – e – n → **hen**
- p – i – g → **pig**
- f – o – x → **fox**
- r – u – g → **rug**
> 💡 Change one sound to make a new word: cat → bat → hat → mat!

## Let's check
Q: Blend: s – i – t.
A: sit.
Q: Which vowel sound is in "cup"?
A: u.
Q: Change the first sound of "pen" to "h". What word do you get?
A: hen.

@@ 3
## Rhyming words
Words that **sound the same at the end** are called rhyming words.

## Rhyming families
- **-at:** cat, bat, hat, mat, rat
- **-en:** hen, pen, ten, men
- **-ig:** pig, big, dig, wig
- **-op:** top, mop, hop, stop
- **-un:** sun, fun, run, bun

## Rhyme in a line
The **cat** sat on a **mat**.
The **pig** is **big**.
The **sun** is **fun**.
> 💡 To find a rhyme, keep the end the same and change the first sound.

## Let's check
Q: Which word rhymes with "hen": pen or cat?
A: Pen.
Q: Give two words that rhyme with "top".
A: Mop, hop or stop.
Q: Does "dog" rhyme with "log"?
A: Yes.

## First sight words
**Sight words** are small words we see very often. We learn to read them at a glance.

## Our sight words
the, a, is, I, am, it, in, on, and, to, see, go, can, we, my, you, he, she, at, up

## Read these sentences
- **I am** a boy. / **I am** a girl.
- **It is** a cat.
- **I can see** the sun.
- **We go to** school.
- The cup **is on** the table.
> 💡 Point to each word with your finger as you read.

## Let's check
Q: Read: "I can see a bus."
A: I can see a bus.
Q: Fill in: "The ball is ___ the box." (in / see)
A: in.

## Listening to stories
A good listener understands the story and can tell it again.

## The Lion and the Mouse
A lion was sleeping in the forest. A little mouse ran over his nose.
The lion woke up and caught the mouse. "Please let me go," said the mouse. "One day I will help you."
The lion laughed and let him go.
A few days later, the lion was caught in a hunter's net. He roared loudly.
The mouse came running. He bit the net with his sharp teeth and set the lion free!

## What did we learn?
Even small friends can be a big help. Be kind to everyone.
> 💡 Tell the story in order: first, then, after that, at the end.

## Let's check
Q: Who woke up the lion?
A: The little mouse.
Q: How did the mouse help the lion?
A: He cut the net with his teeth.
Q: What does the story teach us?
A: Be kind; even small friends can help.
