## Letters make sounds
Every letter makes a sound. When we put sounds together, we make words!

SAY: aarav-wow | The snake says sss… is that the letter S?
SAY: buddy-happy | Yes, Aarav! S says /s/ like a snake. Every letter has its own sound.

## Listen and say
- **A** says "a" as in **a**nt
- **B** says "b" as in **b**at
- **C** says "k" as in **c**up
- **D** says "d" as in **d**rum
- **E** says "e" as in **e**gg
- **F** says "f" as in **f**an
- **G** says "g" as in **g**oat
- **H** says "h" as in **h**en
- **I** says "i" as in **i**nk
- **J** says "j" as in **j**am

## Let's play
Say a sound and find something in your room that starts with it.
"b, b, b… **b**ag!" "f, f, f… **f**an!"
> 💡 Put your hand in front of your mouth and say "h". Can you feel the air?

SAY: riya-happy | B says /b/ for ball, bat and banana!

## Let's check

Q: Which word starts with the "f" sound: fan or cup?

Rhymes are short, happy songs. Words in a rhyme often sound the same at the end, like **star** and **are**.

## Let's talk about pictures
Pictures tell us stories. When we look at a picture, we can say what we see.

SAY: aarav-wow | I see a slide, a tree and a big butterfly!
SAY: buddy-happy | Well done, Aarav! Look carefully and say what you see.

## Look at a park picture
Think of a park. What can you see?
- I see a **tree**. It is green.
- I see a **swing**. A girl is on the swing.
- I see a **dog**. The dog is running.
- I see the **sun**. It is bright.

## Let's say it nicely
Start with "I see a…".
"I see a red ball." "I see a big bird."
> 💡 Use colour words to make your talk better: red, blue, green, yellow.

SAY: riya-happy | We say it nicely: "I can see a red ball."

## Let's do
Look out of your window. Tell your family three things you see.

## Let's check
Q: How do we start talking about a picture?
A: We can say "I see a…".
Q: What colour is a tree's leaf?

## Let's listen to a story
Stories are fun! To enjoy a story, we sit still, look at the person telling it, and listen with our ears.

SAY: riya-think | How will the thirsty crow drink the water?
SAY: buddy-happy | Listen to the story, Riya. The crow has a clever idea!

## The Thirsty Crow
One hot day, a crow was very thirsty.
He found a pot with a little water at the bottom.
His beak could not reach the water.
The crow had an idea! He dropped small stones into the pot, one by one.
The water came up, up, up!
The crow drank the water and flew away happily.

## What did we learn?
- Do not give up.
- Think and you will find a way.
> 💡 Good listeners keep their eyes on the speaker and their hands still.

SAY: aarav-wow | Wow! The clever crow never gave up!

## Let's check
Q: Why was the crow sad?
A: He was thirsty and could not reach the water.
Q: What did the crow put in the pot?
A: Small stones.
Q: What does the story teach us?
A: Do not give up. Think and find a way.
