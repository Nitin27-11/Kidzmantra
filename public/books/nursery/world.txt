SCENE: 🙏 😊 🤝 | Let's learn good manners!

## Good manners
Good manners make everyone happy. 😊

SAY: riya-happy | I always say "please" and "thank you"!
SAY: buddy-happy | Those are magic words, Riya! They make everyone happy.

## Magic words
- Say **"Please"** when you ask for something.
- Say **"Thank you"** when someone helps you.
- Say **"Sorry"** when you make a mistake.
- Say **"Good morning"** and **"Namaste"** to greet people.

## Good habits
- Share your toys.
- Wait for your turn.
- Cover your mouth when you cough or sneeze.
- Throw rubbish in the dustbin.
> 💡 Kind words are like sunshine. They make everyone smile.

SAY: aarav-happy | I say "sorry" when I make a mistake.

## Let's check
Q: What do you say when someone gives you something?
A: Thank you.
Q: What do you say when you make a mistake?
A: Sorry.
Q: What should you do when you sneeze?
A: Cover your mouth and nose.
