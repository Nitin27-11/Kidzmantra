## Our body
Our body is like a wonderful machine. Many parts work together to keep us alive.

SAY: aarav-think | What does my heart do?
SAY: buddy-happy | It pumps blood all over your body, all day and night!

## Inside our body
- **Brain** – controls everything we do and think. It is inside the skull.
- **Heart** – pumps blood to all parts of the body.
- **Lungs** – help us breathe in fresh air.
- **Stomach** – helps digest food.
- **Bones** – give our body shape. Together they make the **skeleton**.
- **Muscles** – help us move.

SAY: riya-wow | We have five sense organs: eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin!

## Sense organs
Eyes (see), ears (hear), nose (smell), tongue (taste), skin (touch).
> 💡 A grown-up has 206 bones!

## Let's check
Q: Which organ pumps blood?
A: The heart.
Q: What do all our bones together make?
A: The skeleton.
Q: Which organ helps us breathe?
A: The lungs.

## Food and health
Food gives us **energy** to work and play, and helps us **grow** and stay **healthy**.

SAY: riya-think | Why should I eat vegetables?
SAY: buddy-happy | They give vitamins and minerals that protect us from illness.

## Kinds of food
- **Energy-giving food:** rice, wheat, potatoes, sugar, ghee.
- **Body-building food:** dal, milk, eggs, paneer, fish.
- **Protective food:** fruits and vegetables – they keep us from falling sick.

SAY: aarav-happy | A balanced diet has all kinds of food in the right amount!

## A balanced diet
A **balanced diet** has all three kinds of food in the right amount, plus water.

## Healthy habits
- Eat fresh food. Cover food to keep flies away.
- Eat less junk food like chips, burgers and fizzy drinks.
- Exercise and play outdoors.
> 💡 Drink 6–8 glasses of water every day.

## Let's check
Q: Name a body-building food.
A: Dal, milk, eggs, paneer or fish.
Q: What is a balanced diet?
A: Food that has all the kinds of food we need in the right amounts.
Q: Why should we cover food?
A: To keep flies and germs away.

SCENE: 🌱 🌳 ☀️ | Plants!

## Plants
Plants are living things. They make their own food using sunlight, air and water.

SAY: aarav-wow | Roots drink water from the soil!
SAY: buddy-happy | Yes! And leaves make food for the plant using sunlight.

## Parts of a plant and their work
- **Root** – holds the plant in the soil and takes in water.
- **Stem** – holds the plant up and carries water to the leaves.
- **Leaves** – make food for the plant using sunlight.
- **Flower** – becomes the fruit.
- **Fruit** – has seeds inside.
- **Seed** – grows into a new plant.

SAY: riya-happy | Plants need water, air and sunlight to grow.

## What plants need
Sunlight, water, air and soil.

## Plants we eat
- Roots: carrot, radish
- Stem: sugarcane, potato
- Leaves: spinach, cabbage
- Seeds: wheat, rice, peas
> 💡 Leaves are green because of a green colour called chlorophyll.

## Let's check
Q: Which part of the plant makes food?
A: The leaves.
Q: What do plants need to grow?
A: Sunlight, water, air and soil.
Q: Which part of sugarcane do we eat?
A: The stem.

SCENE: 🐄 🦁 🐝 | The world of animals!

## Animals
Animals are living things. They need food, water and shelter.

SAY: riya-think | Why does a lion eat meat and a cow eat grass?
SAY: buddy-happy | Animals eat different food. Plant-eaters are herbivores, meat-eaters are carnivores.

## Kinds of animals
- **Domestic animals** live with us: cow, dog, goat.
- **Wild animals** live in forests: tiger, deer, bear.

## Animals by food
- **Herbivores** eat plants: cow, horse, deer.
- **Carnivores** eat other animals: lion, tiger, eagle.
- **Omnivores** eat both: bear, crow, human.

SAY: aarav-happy | Bees give us honey and sheep give us wool!

## Animals give us
Milk (cow, goat, buffalo), eggs (hen), wool (sheep), honey (bees), silk (silkworm).
> 💡 Animals with backbones are called vertebrates. Fish, frogs, birds and humans all have backbones.

## Let's check
Q: What is a herbivore?
A: An animal that eats only plants.
Q: Which animal gives us wool?
A: The sheep.
Q: Is a bear a herbivore, carnivore or omnivore?
A: An omnivore.

SCENE: 💧 🌊 🧊 | Water!

## Water
Water is needed by all living things. We cannot live without water.

SAY: aarav-happy | We get water from rivers, wells and rain!
SAY: buddy-happy | Yes! And water can be ice, liquid water or steam.

## Sources of water
- **Rain** – the main source.
- **Rivers, lakes and ponds**.
- **Wells, tube wells and hand pumps** – water from under the ground.
- **Sea** – salty water we cannot drink.

## Forms of water
- **Solid** – ice
- **Liquid** – water
- **Gas** – steam (water vapour)

## Clean water
Make water safe by **boiling**, **filtering** or using a **water purifier**.

SAY: riya-happy | I close the tap tightly to save water.

## Save water
Turn off taps, use a bucket to bathe, reuse water from washing vegetables to water plants.
> 💡 Most of Earth is covered with water, but very little of it is fresh water we can drink.

## Let's check
Q: Name the three forms of water.
A: Ice (solid), water (liquid) and steam (gas).
Q: Why can't we drink sea water?
A: Because it is salty.
Q: Give two ways to make water safe to drink.
A: Boiling and filtering.

SCENE: 🌬️ 🪁 🎈 | Air is everywhere!

## Air
Air is all around us. We cannot see it, but we can **feel** it.

SAY: riya-think | I can't see air. How do I know it's there?
SAY: buddy-happy | Watch a kite fly or a balloon fill up. That's air!

## Air is everywhere
When the wind blows, leaves move and clothes on the line flutter. A balloon fills up with air.

## Uses of air
- We **breathe** air to stay alive.
- Air dries wet clothes.
- Air helps kites fly and windmills turn.
- Moving air (wind) moves sailing boats.

SAY: aarav-happy | Trees help keep the air fresh and clean.

## Fresh air
Plants give out fresh air. Smoke from cars and factories makes air dirty. This is called **air pollution**.

## Keep air clean
Plant trees, walk or cycle for short distances, and never burn rubbish.
> 💡 Moving air is called wind. Very strong wind is called a storm.

## Let's check
Q: Can we see air?
A: No, but we can feel it.
Q: Name two uses of air.
A: Breathing, drying clothes, flying kites, turning windmills.
Q: How do plants help keep air clean?
A: They give out fresh air (oxygen).

## Festivals of India
India has people of many religions and cultures. We celebrate many festivals together.

SAY: riya-happy | We celebrate Diwali, Eid, Christmas and Gurpurab!
SAY: buddy-happy | India has many religious festivals, and everyone celebrates together.

## Religious festivals
- **Diwali** – festival of lights; we light diyas and share sweets.
- **Holi** – festival of colours; it marks the start of spring.
- **Eid-ul-Fitr** – celebrated after the month of Ramzan with prayers and sewaiyan.
- **Christmas** – birthday of Jesus Christ, on 25 December.
- **Guru Nanak Jayanti** – birthday of Guru Nanak Dev Ji.
- **Buddha Purnima** – birthday of Lord Buddha.

SAY: aarav-wow | Pongal, Baisakhi and Onam are harvest festivals!

## Harvest festivals
Pongal (Tamil Nadu), Onam (Kerala), Baisakhi (Punjab), Bihu (Assam), Lohri, Makar Sankranti.

## National festivals
Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October).
> 💡 Festivals bring people together. We should respect every festival.

## Let's check
Q: When is Christmas celebrated?
A: On 25 December.
Q: Name a harvest festival.
A: Pongal, Onam, Baisakhi, Bihu or Lohri.
Q: When is Republic Day?
A: 26 January.

## Transport
**Transport** means moving people and goods from one place to another.

SAY: aarav-happy | I go to school by bicycle!
SAY: buddy-happy | Land, water and air – there are three kinds of transport.

## Land transport
Bicycle, scooter, car, bus, truck, train, auto-rickshaw, bullock cart.

## Water transport
Boat, ship, steamer, ferry. Ships carry goods across the seas.

## Air transport
Aeroplane and helicopter. Air travel is the fastest.

SAY: riya-think | Long ago, people used bullock carts and horses!

## Old and new
Long ago, people walked or used animals like horses, camels and elephants. Now we have fast trains like the metro and Vande Bharat.
> 💡 The camel is called the "ship of the desert" because it carries people across sand.

## Let's check
Q: Which transport is the fastest?
A: Air transport (aeroplane).
Q: Which animal is called the ship of the desert?
A: The camel.
Q: Name two kinds of water transport.
A: Boat and ship.

SCENE: 🚦 🦺 ⚠️ | Safety first!

## Safety rules
Following safety rules protects us from accidents.

SAY: riya-happy | We cross the road at a zebra crossing!
SAY: buddy-happy | Yes! And always look right, left and right again.

## Safety on the road
- Walk on the **footpath**.
- Cross at the **zebra crossing** when the signal is green for walkers.
- Look right, left, then right again before crossing.
- Wear a **helmet** on a two-wheeler and a **seat belt** in a car.

SAY: aarav-think | I shouldn't touch electric switches with wet hands!

## Safety at home
- Do not touch electric switches with wet hands.
- Stay away from fire, the gas stove and sharp knives.
- Do not take medicines without an adult.

## Safety at school
- Do not push others on the stairs.
- Play safely in the playground.
> 💡 Emergency numbers in India: 112 (all emergencies), 101 (fire), 108 (ambulance).

## Let's check
Q: Where should we cross the road?
A: At the zebra crossing.
Q: Why should we not touch switches with wet hands?
A: We can get an electric shock.
Q: Which number do we call for any emergency in India?
A: 112.
