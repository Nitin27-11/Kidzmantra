## My body and senses
We have **five senses**. They help us know what is around us.

SAY: aarav-wow | I can smell Mummy's halwa from far away!
SAY: buddy-happy | That's your nose! We see, hear, smell, taste and touch.

## The five senses
- **Eyes** help us **see** colours and shapes. 👀
- **Ears** help us **hear** sounds and music. 👂
- **Nose** helps us **smell** flowers and food. 👃
- **Tongue** helps us **taste** sweet, salty and sour. 👅
- **Skin** helps us **feel** hot, cold, soft and hard. ✋

## Let's play
Close your eyes. Can you tell what you are holding by touching it?
Smell a flower and a lemon. Which smell do you like?
> 💡 Never look straight at the sun. It can hurt your eyes.

SAY: riya-happy | I touch a soft teddy with my hands.

## Let's check
Q: Which part helps us taste?
A: The tongue.
Q: How many senses do we have?
A: Five.
Q: With which sense do we hear music?
A: Hearing, with our ears.

## Domestic and wild animals
Some animals live with people. Some live in forests. Let's learn both!

SAY: aarav-wow | A lion lives in the forest! Can I keep it at home?
SAY: buddy-happy | No, Aarav! Lions are wild animals. Cows and dogs live with us.

## Domestic animals
Domestic animals live near people and help us.
- **Cow** gives us milk. 🐄
- **Hen** gives us eggs. 🐔
- **Dog** guards our home. 🐶
- **Horse** and **ox** carry loads.

## Wild animals
Wild animals live in forests and jungles.
- **Lion** – king of the jungle. 🦁
- **Tiger** – India's national animal. 🐯
- **Elephant** – the biggest land animal. 🐘
- **Monkey**, **bear** and **deer** also live in forests.
> 💡 We see wild animals safely in a zoo or a national park.

SAY: riya-happy | The cow gives us milk!

## Let's check
Q: Which animal gives us milk?
A: The cow.
Q: What is India's national animal?
A: The tiger.
Q: Is a lion domestic or wild?
A: Wild.

## Birds around us
Birds have **feathers**, **wings** and a **beak**. Most birds can fly. 🐦

SAY: riya-wow | A green parrot is sitting on our tree!
SAY: buddy-happy | Birds have wings and feathers. Most birds can fly!

## Birds we see
- **Sparrow** – small and brown. It says "chirp chirp".
- **Crow** – black and clever. It says "caw caw".
- **Parrot** – green with a red beak. It can copy words.
- **Pigeon** – grey. It says "coo coo".
- **Peacock** – India's national bird. It dances in the rain. 🦚

## Birds' homes
Birds build **nests** with twigs, grass and straw. They lay eggs in their nests.
> 💡 Keep a bowl of water out for birds in summer.

SAY: aarav-happy | Birds build nests to keep their eggs safe.

## Let's check
Q: What is India's national bird?
A: The peacock.
Q: What is a bird's home called?
A: A nest.
Q: Which bird says "caw caw"?
A: The crow.

## Fruits and vegetables
Fruits and vegetables keep us healthy and strong. 🥕🍎

SAY: aarav-happy | Carrots are orange and crunchy!
SAY: buddy-happy | Vegetables and fruits give us energy to play.

## Fruits
Mango, banana, apple, grapes, papaya, guava and orange.
Most fruits are sweet and can be eaten raw.

## Vegetables
Potato, tomato, carrot, onion, spinach, peas and cauliflower.
Many vegetables are cooked before we eat them.

## Grows where?
- Some grow **on trees**: mango, coconut.
- Some grow **under the ground**: potato, carrot, onion.
- Some grow **on plants**: tomato, brinjal.
> 💡 Green leafy vegetables like spinach make us strong.

SAY: riya-wow | Potatoes grow under the ground!

## Let's check
Q: Name a vegetable that grows under the ground.
A: Potato, carrot or onion.
Q: Name a fruit that grows on a tree.
A: Mango or coconut.
Q: Is a carrot a fruit or a vegetable?
A: A vegetable.

## Means of transport
Transport means ways of going from one place to another.

SAY: riya-happy | We went to Nani's house by train!
SAY: buddy-happy | We travel on land, on water and in the air.

## Land transport
Bicycle, car, bus, auto-rickshaw, train, bullock cart.

## Water transport
Boat, ship, ferry.

## Air transport
Aeroplane, helicopter.

## Road safety
- Cross the road at the **zebra crossing**.
- **Red** light means stop, **yellow** means get ready, **green** means go. 🚦
- Hold a grown-up's hand on the road.
> 💡 Look right, look left, and look right again before crossing.

SAY: aarav-happy | We cross the road only when the light is green for us!

## Let's check
Q: Name two kinds of water transport.
A: Boat and ship.
Q: What does a red light mean?
A: Stop.
Q: Where should we cross the road?
A: At the zebra crossing.

SCENE: 🪔 🎨 🇮🇳 | Festivals of India!

## Festivals of India
India has many festivals. We celebrate with family and friends! 🎉

SAY: riya-wow | We light diyas on Diwali!
SAY: buddy-happy | India has many festivals: Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Holi and more.

## Some festivals
- **Diwali** – festival of lights. We light diyas. 🪔
- **Holi** – festival of colours. 🎨
- **Eid** – we share sweets like sewaiyan and hug friends.
- **Christmas** – we decorate a tree and sing carols. 🎄
- **Guru Nanak Jayanti** – we remember Guru Nanak Dev Ji and share langar.
- **Pongal** and **Onam** – harvest festivals of South India.

## National festivals
- **Independence Day** – 15 August.
- **Republic Day** – 26 January.
- **Gandhi Jayanti** – 2 October.
> 💡 Festivals teach us to share, love and be happy together.

SAY: aarav-happy | On 15 August, we fly our flag!

## Let's check
Q: Which festival is called the festival of lights?
A: Diwali.
Q: Which festival is the festival of colours?
A: Holi.
Q: When is Independence Day?
A: 15 August.
