## Alphabet and phonics
English has **26 letters**: 5 vowels (a, e, i, o, u) and 21 consonants. Each letter has a name and a sound.

SAY: riya-think | Why do I need to know letter sounds?
SAY: buddy-happy | Because sounds join to make words! c-a-t makes cat.

## Letter sounds we use to read
- c-a-t → **cat**, d-o-g → **dog**, s-u-n → **sun**
- When two letters work together they can make one new sound:
- **sh** as in **sh**ip, **sh**op
- **ch** as in **ch**air, **ch**ick
- **th** as in **th**ree, **th**is

SAY: aarav-wow | The a in "cake" says its own name! That's a long vowel!

## Long vowel sounds
When a word ends in a silent **e**, the vowel says its name:
- c**a**p → c**a**pe
- k**i**t → k**i**te
- h**o**p → h**o**pe
> 💡 Magic "e" is quiet, but it makes the vowel say its own name!

## Let's practise
Read: ship, chin, thin, cake, bike, rose.

SAY: buddy-happy | Practise a little every day and you will read on your own!

## Let's check
Q: Which two letters make the sound in "ship"?
A: sh.
Q: Add magic e to "kit". What word do you get?
A: kite.
Q: Is "o" a vowel or a consonant?
A: A vowel.

## Naming words
A **naming word** is the name of a person, animal, place or thing. It is also called a **noun**.

SAY: aarav-think | Is "Aarav" a naming word too?
SAY: buddy-happy | Yes! Names of people, animals, places and things are all naming words.

## Examples
- **Person:** mother, teacher, Riya, doctor
- **Animal:** cow, parrot, tiger
- **Place:** school, park, Delhi, market
- **Thing:** bag, pencil, chair, book

## Special names
Names of particular people and places start with a capital letter: **Aman**, **Mumbai**, **India**.

SAY: riya-happy | Special names like Delhi and Riya start with a capital letter!

## Find the naming words
- The **dog** is in the **garden**.
- **Meena** has a red **ball**.
> 💡 Ask "Who?" or "What?". The answer is often a naming word.

## Let's check
Q: Find the naming words: "The cat sat on the mat."
A: cat, mat.
Q: Is "school" a person, animal, place or thing?
A: A place.
Q: Why does "Delhi" start with a capital letter?
A: It is the special name of a place.

## Action words
An **action word** tells us what someone or something **does**. It is also called a **verb**.

SAY: riya-happy | I run, I jump, I sing! These are all action words!
SAY: buddy-happy | Yes! Action words tell us what someone does.

## Examples
run, jump, eat, sleep, read, write, sing, dance, swim, fly, cry, laugh

## In sentences
- The baby **cries**.
- Birds **fly**.
- I **read** a book.
- Raju **kicks** the ball.

SAY: aarav-wow | Let's act it out: I am clapping! You guess my action word!

## Act it out
Play "Simon says": "Simon says **jump**!" "Simon says **clap**!"
> 💡 Ask "What is he doing?" The answer is an action word.

## Let's check
Q: Find the action word: "The fish swims."
A: swims.
Q: Give two action words you do every morning.
A: Brush, bathe, eat, walk, etc.
Q: Is "pencil" an action word?
A: No, it is a naming word.

## Simple sentences
A **sentence** is a group of words that makes complete sense.

SAY: aarav-think | Is "the big red" a sentence?
SAY: buddy-happy | No, it doesn't make full sense. A sentence tells a complete idea.

## Rules for a sentence
1. It starts with a **capital letter**.
2. It ends with a **full stop (.)** or a **question mark (?)**.
3. The words are in the right order.

## Examples
- This is a red ball.
- I like mangoes.
- Where is my bag?

## Jumbled words
"is / sun / The / hot" → **The sun is hot.**
"a / I / have / kite" → **I have a kite.**
> 💡 Leave a finger space between words when you write.

SAY: riya-happy | A sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop!

## Let's check
Q: Make a sentence: "dog / The / barks".
A: The dog barks.
Q: What do we put at the end of a question?
A: A question mark (?).
Q: Is "red big ball" a sentence?
A: No, it does not make complete sense.

## Reading short stories
Reading stories helps us learn new words and ideas.

SAY: riya-wow | Tara's seed grew into a yellow flower!
SAY: buddy-happy | It needed water, sunlight and care every day.

## The Little Seed
Tara planted a small seed in a pot.
Every day she gave it water and kept it in the sun.
One morning, she saw a tiny green shoot!
Day by day, it grew taller.
After many weeks, a beautiful yellow flower bloomed.
Tara was very happy. "Thank you, little plant!" she said.

## New words
- **seed** – a tiny thing that grows into a plant
- **shoot** – the first small stem of a new plant
- **bloomed** – opened into a flower

SAY: aarav-happy | I will read the story again to understand it better!

## How to read well
Read slowly. Look at each word. Read the story again to understand it better.
> 💡 Plants need water, sunlight and care to grow – just like children need food, sleep and love!

## Let's check
Q: What did Tara plant?
A: A small seed.
Q: What did she give the plant every day?
A: Water and sunlight.
Q: What colour was the flower?
A: Yellow.

## Poems and rhymes
A **poem** is a piece of writing with rhythm. Many poems have rhyming words.

SAY: aarav-wow | The moon is a silver ball in the poem!
SAY: buddy-happy | Poems have rhythm. Bright and night rhyme – they sound alike!

## The Moon
The moon is round, the moon is bright,
It shines on me at night, at night.
Sometimes it is a silver ball,
Sometimes it is not there at all!

## Rhyming pairs in the poem
- bright – night
- ball – all

## Enjoying a poem
Read it aloud. Clap the beat. Act out the words.
> 💡 The moon changes its shape every night. These shapes are called phases.

SAY: riya-happy | I clapped the beat while reading. So much fun!

## Let's check
Q: Which word rhymes with "bright"?
A: night.
Q: What does the poem compare the moon to?
A: A silver ball.
Q: Give a rhyming word for "ball".
A: all, tall, wall or call.
