## Myself
I am special. There is nobody else exactly like me!

## All about me
- I have a **name**.
- I have a **birthday**.
- I am a boy or a girl, and I study in Class 1.
- I have likes: my favourite colour, food and game.

## I am growing
When I was a baby, I could not walk or talk. Now I can run, read and write! I grow taller every year.

## I take care of myself
- I bathe and brush my teeth every day.
- I eat healthy food and drink water.
- I keep my things neat.
> 💡 Learn your full name, your parents' names and your home address – but share them only with trusted people.

## Let's check
Q: Name two things you can do now that you could not do as a baby.
A: Walk, talk, read, write, run, etc.
Q: Should you tell your address to a stranger?
A: No.

## My family
My family loves me and takes care of me. I love my family too.

## Family members
Mother, father, brothers, sisters, grandparents, uncles, aunts and cousins.

## Kinds of families
- **Nuclear family** – parents and children.
- **Joint family** – grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts and children living together.

## How we help
- Mother and father work and look after the home.
- Grandparents tell stories and teach us good things.
- Children help by keeping things in place and helping in small jobs.
> 💡 We celebrate festivals and birthdays together as a family.

## Let's check
Q: What is a joint family?
A: A family where grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts and children live together.
Q: How can you help at home?
A: Keep toys in place, water plants, set the table, etc.

@@ 3
## My home
A home protects us from the sun, rain, cold and animals. 🏠

## Kinds of houses
- **Pucca house** – made of bricks, cement and iron. Strong.
- **Kutcha house** – made of mud, straw and wood.
- **Flat** – homes one above the other in a tall building.
- **Houseboat** – a home on water, like in Kashmir.
- **Tent** – easy to carry; used by travellers and soldiers.

## Rooms and their uses
Kitchen for cooking, bedroom for sleeping, bathroom for bathing, living room for sitting together.

## A clean home
A clean home keeps away flies, mosquitoes and diseases.
> 💡 An igloo is a house made of ice blocks in very cold places.

## Let's check
Q: Which house is made of bricks and cement?
A: A pucca house.
Q: What is a home on water called?
A: A houseboat.
Q: Why should we keep our home clean?
A: To keep away flies, mosquitoes and diseases.

@@ 4
## Plants around us
Plants are living things. They grow, need food and water, and give us many things.

## Kinds of plants
- **Trees** – big and tall with a hard, thick trunk. Mango, neem, banyan.
- **Shrubs** – smaller with many woody stems. Rose, tulsi, hibiscus.
- **Herbs** – small, soft-stemmed plants. Mint, coriander, grass.
- **Climbers** – weak stems that climb up support. Money plant, grapevine.
- **Creepers** – weak stems that spread on the ground. Pumpkin, watermelon.

## Parts of a plant
Root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit and seed.

## Plants give us
Fruits, vegetables, wood, cotton, medicines and fresh air.
> 💡 Never pluck leaves and flowers for fun. Plants are living things too.

## Let's check
Q: Name a climber.
A: Money plant or grapevine.
Q: Which part of a plant holds it in the soil?
A: The root.
Q: Name two things plants give us.
A: Fruits, vegetables, wood, cotton, medicines, fresh air.

@@ 5
## Animals around us
There are many kinds of animals. Some live with us; some live in forests; some live in water.

## Where they live
- **Pet animals:** dog, cat, parrot – live in our homes.
- **Farm animals:** cow, buffalo, goat, hen – live on farms.
- **Wild animals:** lion, tiger, elephant – live in forests.
- **Water animals:** fish, whale, octopus – live in water.

## What they eat
- **Plant-eaters:** cow, goat, rabbit, elephant.
- **Meat-eaters:** lion, tiger, wolf.
- **Eat both:** bear, crow, dog.

## How they move
Birds fly, fish swim, snakes crawl, frogs hop, horses run.
> 💡 Be kind to animals. Never tease or hurt them.

## Let's check
Q: Name two farm animals.
A: Cow, buffalo, goat or hen.
Q: What does a lion eat?
A: Meat (it is a meat-eater).
Q: How does a frog move?
A: It hops (and swims).

@@ 6
## Food we eat
We need food to grow, to be strong and to have energy to play. 🍽️

## Food from plants
Rice, wheat, dal, fruits, vegetables, sugar, oil.

## Food from animals
Milk, curd, paneer, ghee, honey, eggs, fish.

## Our meals
- **Breakfast** in the morning – poha, idli, paratha, milk.
- **Lunch** in the afternoon – dal, rice, roti, sabzi.
- **Dinner** at night.

## Good eating habits
- Wash hands before eating.
- Chew food properly.
- Do not waste food.
- Eat less junk food like chips and sweets.
> 💡 Different parts of India eat different foods: dosa in the South, makki di roti in Punjab, fish curry in Bengal.

## Let's check
Q: Name two foods we get from animals.
A: Milk, curd, paneer, eggs, honey, fish.
Q: Name one good eating habit.
A: Wash hands, chew properly, do not waste food.

@@ 7
## Water
Water is very important. All living things need water to live. 💧

## Uses of water
Drinking, cooking, bathing, washing clothes, cleaning the house, watering plants.

## Where we get water
Rain, rivers, lakes, ponds, wells, taps and hand pumps.

## Clean water
Drink only **clean** water. Dirty water can make us sick. Water can be cleaned by **boiling** or using a **filter**.

## Save water
- Close the tap while brushing.
- Use a bucket instead of a shower.
- Fix leaking taps.
> 💡 Every drop counts! A leaking tap can waste many buckets of water in a day.

## Let's check
Q: Name two sources of water.
A: Rain, river, lake, pond, well, tap.
Q: How can we make water clean to drink?
A: By boiling it or using a filter.
Q: Give one way to save water.
A: Close the tap while brushing.

## Our helpers
Many people work hard to make our lives easy. They are our **helpers**.

## Helpers and their work
- **Doctor and nurse** – look after us when we are sick.
- **Teacher** – helps us learn.
- **Farmer** – grows food.
- **Police officer** – keeps us safe.
- **Postman** – delivers letters and parcels.
- **Carpenter** – makes furniture from wood.
- **Tailor** – stitches clothes.
- **Cobbler** – mends shoes.
- **Sweeper** – keeps our streets clean.

## Their tools
Doctor – stethoscope, carpenter – saw and hammer, tailor – needle and scissors, farmer – plough.
> 💡 Every job is important. Respect all helpers.

## Let's check
Q: Who makes furniture?
A: A carpenter.
Q: What tool does a doctor use to listen to our heartbeat?
A: A stethoscope.
Q: Who mends shoes?
A: A cobbler.
