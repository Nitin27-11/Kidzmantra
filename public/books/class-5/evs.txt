## How body systems work together
Our body is made of tiny units called **cells**. Cells form **tissues**, tissues form **organs**, and organs work together in **systems**.

## Main systems
- **Skeletal system:** 206 bones support the body and protect organs. Joints let us move.
- **Muscular system:** muscles pull bones to make movement. The heart is also a muscle.
- **Digestive system:** mouth → food pipe → stomach → small intestine → large intestine. Breaks food into nutrients.
- **Respiratory system:** nose → windpipe → lungs. We take in oxygen and give out carbon dioxide.
- **Circulatory system:** the heart pumps blood through **arteries** (away from the heart) and **veins** (back to the heart). Blood carries oxygen and food to every cell.
- **Nervous system:** brain, spinal cord and nerves. The brain is the control centre.
- **Excretory system:** kidneys filter waste from the blood and make urine.

## Teamwork example
When you run: the brain sends signals, muscles move bones, lungs breathe faster, and the heart beats faster to send more oxygen.
> 💡 Your heart beats about one lakh times a day!

## Nutrients in food
Food has **nutrients** that our body needs:
- **Carbohydrates** – give energy: rice, wheat, potatoes, sugar.
- **Fats** – give lots of energy and keep us warm: ghee, butter, oil, nuts.
- **Proteins** – build and repair the body: dal, milk, eggs, paneer, fish, soybean.
- **Vitamins** – keep us healthy: fruits and vegetables.
- **Minerals** – iron for blood, calcium for bones and teeth: green vegetables, milk.
- **Water and roughage (fibre)** – help digestion.

SAY: riya-think | Why do some children get weak bones?
SAY: buddy-happy | Without enough calcium and vitamin D, bones can become weak.

## Deficiency diseases
When we don't get enough of a nutrient, we get **deficiency diseases**:
- Lack of **vitamin A** → night blindness (eat carrots, papaya).
- Lack of **vitamin C** → scurvy, bleeding gums (eat amla, oranges, lemons).
- Lack of **vitamin D** → rickets, weak bones (sit in morning sunlight).
- Lack of **iron** → anaemia (eat spinach, jaggery).
- Lack of **iodine** → goitre (use iodised salt).

SAY: aarav-happy | A balanced diet keeps deficiency diseases away!

## Balanced diet
A balanced diet has all nutrients in the right amounts.
> 💡 Sunlight helps our skin make vitamin D.

## Germs and diseases
**Germs** (microbes) are tiny living things seen only through a microscope. Some cause diseases.

SAY: aarav-think | How does a cold spread?
SAY: buddy-happy | Through droplets when people cough or sneeze. Cover your mouth!

## How diseases spread
- **Air:** cold, flu, tuberculosis – through coughs and sneezes.
- **Water and food:** cholera, typhoid, jaundice – from dirty water and uncovered food.
- **Insects:** mosquitoes spread **malaria** and **dengue**; flies carry germs to food.
- **Contact:** some skin infections spread by touching.

## Preventing diseases
- Wash hands with soap.
- Drink clean, boiled or filtered water.
- Don't let water collect around the house – mosquitoes breed in still water.
- Use mosquito nets and repellents.
- Get **vaccinated** – vaccines protect us from diseases like polio, measles and tetanus.

SAY: riya-happy | Diabetes doesn't spread from person to person – it's non-communicable!

## Communicable and non-communicable
- **Communicable:** spread from person to person (flu, chickenpox).
- **Non-communicable:** don't spread (diabetes, heart disease).
> 💡 India was declared polio-free in 2014 thanks to vaccination drives.

## Parts of a seed
- **Seed coat:** the protective outer cover.
- **Cotyledons:** store food for the baby plant. Bean and pea seeds have two (**dicots**); maize and wheat have one (**monocots**).
- **Embryo:** the baby plant, with a tiny root (**radicle**) and shoot (**plumule**).

SAY: buddy-happy | When it gets water, air and warmth, it germinates.

## Germination
A seed needs **water**, **air** and the right **temperature** to germinate. Soil is not needed at first – seeds sprout on wet cotton!
1. The seed absorbs water and swells.
2. The radicle grows down into a root.
3. The plumule grows up into a shoot.
4. First leaves appear and the plant starts making its own food.

SAY: aarav-wow | A potato can grow a new plant from its eyes!

## Other ways plants grow
Not all plants grow from seeds:
- **Stem cuttings:** rose, money plant, sugarcane.
- **Leaves:** bryophyllum grows new plants from its leaf edges.
- **Tubers:** potato "eyes" sprout into new plants.
> 💡 Farmers soak seeds overnight to help them germinate faster.

A: Water, air and the right temperature.

A: Rose, money plant or sugarcane.

## Super senses in animals
Animals have sharp senses that help them find food and stay safe.
- **Sight:** eagles and hawks spot prey from great heights. Owls see well at night.
- **Smell:** dogs track people by smell; ants follow scent trails.
- **Hearing:** dogs hear sounds we cannot; elephants communicate with very low sounds.
- **Echolocation:** bats and dolphins make sounds and listen for echoes to "see" in the dark.
- **Touch:** a cat's whiskers sense the width of openings.

SAY: buddy-happy | And animals adapt to their homes – like polar bears with thick fur.
SAY: riya-think | Bats find their way in the dark using sound echoes!

## Adaptations
Special body features and behaviours help animals survive in their habitats.
- **Camouflage:** chameleons change colour; tigers' stripes hide them in tall grass.
- **Hibernation:** some animals, like frogs and bears, sleep through the cold season.
- **Migration:** birds like the Siberian crane fly thousands of kilometres to India in winter.
- **Desert:** camels have humps of fat, long eyelashes and wide feet for sand.
- **Water:** ducks have webbed feet and oily feathers.
> 💡 Bharatpur bird sanctuary in Rajasthan welcomes many migratory birds every winter.

A: By echolocation – listening for echoes of their sounds.
Q: What is camouflage?
A: Blending with surroundings to hide.
Q: What is migration?

## Why we must save water
Only about **3%** of Earth's water is fresh, and most of it is frozen as ice. India's growing population and cities need more water every year. Many places face **water scarcity**.

## Wasting water
- Leaking taps and pipes
- Keeping taps open while brushing
- Over-watering gardens
- Polluting rivers with waste and chemicals

## Ways to save water
- **Rainwater harvesting:** collect rain from rooftops into tanks or send it underground to refill groundwater.
- **Drip irrigation:** gives water drop by drop to plant roots – saves a lot of water in farming.
- **Reuse:** water used to wash vegetables can water plants.
- Fix leaks; take bucket baths; turn off taps.

SAY: aarav-wow | Rajasthan's stepwells stored water for hundreds of years!

## Traditional water systems in India
**Stepwells (baolis)**, **johads** (Rajasthan), **kuhls** (Himachal) and **tanks** (South India) show how people stored water long ago.
> 💡 22 March is celebrated as World Water Day.

## Let's check

A: Collecting and storing rainwater for later use or to refill groundwater.
Q: How does drip irrigation save water?
A: It gives water drop by drop directly to plant roots.
Q: Name one traditional water system in India.
A: Stepwell (baoli), johad, kuhl or tank.

## What makes air dirty?
**Air pollution** happens when harmful gases, smoke and dust mix with air.

SAY: aarav-think | Where does dirty air come from?
SAY: buddy-happy | Smoke from vehicles, factories and burning waste.

## Sources
- Smoke from vehicles and factories
- Burning garbage, crop stubble and firewood
- Dust from construction
- Bursting crackers

## Effects
- Breathing problems like **asthma**, coughing and burning eyes.
- **Smog** – a mix of smoke and fog – makes it hard to see.
- **Acid rain** damages buildings and plants; it has harmed the marble of the Taj Mahal.
- **Global warming:** too much carbon dioxide traps heat and warms the Earth.

SAY: riya-happy | We can walk, cycle and plant more trees!

## How we can help
- Walk, cycle or use public transport.
- Plant trees – they absorb carbon dioxide and give oxygen.
- Use CNG and electric vehicles.
- Don't burn garbage; segregate it.
> 💡 Check the AQI (Air Quality Index) – "good" is 0–50.

## Let's check
Q: What is smog?
A: A mixture of smoke and fog.
Q: How do trees help keep air clean?
A: They absorb carbon dioxide and give out oxygen.
Q: Name two sources of air pollution.
A: Vehicle smoke, factory smoke, burning garbage, construction dust.

The **solar system** is the Sun and everything that moves around it.
- **The Sun** is a star – a huge ball of burning gases. It gives light and heat.
- **Eight planets** go around the Sun: **Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.**

## Planet facts
- Mercury is closest to the Sun; Venus is the hottest and brightest.
- Earth is the only planet known to have life.
- Mars is the "red planet".
- Jupiter is the biggest; Saturn has beautiful rings.

## Earth's movements
- **Rotation:** Earth spins on its axis once every **24 hours** → day and night.

The Moon is Earth's natural **satellite**. It has no light of its own; it reflects sunlight.

## Natural disasters
A **natural disaster** is a sudden event in nature that causes great damage.
- **Earthquake:** the ground shakes because of movement deep inside the Earth.
- **Flood:** too much rain makes rivers overflow.
- **Cyclone:** a huge, spinning storm with strong winds and heavy rain, coming from the sea.
- **Drought:** no rain for a long time; crops fail and water dries up.
- **Landslide:** rocks and soil slide down hills, often during heavy rain.
- **Tsunami:** giant sea waves caused by an undersea earthquake.

SAY: aarav-think | What should I do in an earthquake?
SAY: buddy-happy | Drop, cover and hold on – under a strong table, away from windows.

## Staying safe
- **Earthquake:** Drop, Cover and Hold On – get under a strong table, away from windows.
- **Flood:** move to higher ground; don't walk through flowing water.
- **Cyclone:** stay indoors, listen to weather warnings.

SAY: riya-happy | Keeping an emergency kit ready helps a lot!

## Help and preparation
The **NDRF** (National Disaster Response Force) helps people during disasters. Keep an emergency kit: torch, water, first-aid box, dry food, important papers.
> 💡 Weather satellites help warn people about cyclones days in advance.

## Forests are precious
Forests give us oxygen, rain, wood, fruits, medicines, honey and homes for animals. Tree roots hold soil and prevent floods and soil erosion.

SAY: riya-happy | Many tribes live in the forest and take care of it!

## Forest people
**Tribal communities** like the Gonds, Bhils, Santhals and Todas have lived in forests for generations. They depend on forests for food, medicine and shelter, and they protect them. They take only what they need.

SAY: buddy-happy | Cutting too many trees – deforestation – harms animals and people.

## Deforestation
Cutting down forests for farms, roads, factories and towns is called **deforestation**. It causes:
- Loss of animal homes; some animals become **endangered**.
- Less rain and more floods.
- More carbon dioxide in the air.

SAY: aarav-happy | Remember the Chipko movement? People hugged trees to save them!

## Saving forests
- **Chipko Movement (1973):** villagers in Uttarakhand hugged trees to stop them from being cut.
- **Van Mahotsav:** a tree-planting festival in July.
- Use less paper; recycle.
> 💡 One big tree can give enough oxygen for several people every day.

A: Cutting down forests on a large scale.
